# Repository casebook

Status: evidence from Anders's projects, not general instructions. Inspect the
live repository before reusing a pattern. A pattern belongs here even when the
lesson is that it was premature or drifted.

## Small knowledge projects

**Training** is evidence that a young project can remain shallow. Its durable
background, current plan, activity areas, and concerns can begin in combined
owners. A reported workout can become a date-marked session record immediately;
more sessions can later justify an activity area, index, schema, or application.
The useful transition is to split only after use exposes a distinct question,
as happened when swimming technique developed enough content to need its own
treatment. Anticipated source, output, decision, and tracker structures remain
unproven and should not be copied in advance.

**Cooking** proved the date-marked narrative record for a completed cook. It
separates that lived result from the reusable method, which the evidence can
later improve without pretending one outcome was a general law. It also shows
that a retired but obvious path may deserve a thin signpost. Its planned
raw-transcript and compiled-note source architecture is still mostly a proposal;
empty pantry and transcript structures are evidence against scaffolding
taxonomies before use.

## Knowledge and administration

**MIT** grew from one exchange domain into administration, courses, and term
modules as each acquired different decisions and state. Per-course notes own
course knowledge while generated Obsidian views provide alternate routes.
Preference, feasibility, formal approval, registration, and completion are
different questions; overloading them into one status creates drift. MIT uses
source layers selectively where email, social, or official evidence benefits
from a transformation trail, not for every Markdown source.

**Hub** distinguishes durable intent from volatile machine state and uses maps,
work records, evidence assets, safety gates, and later operational code. It also
shows the failure modes: stale state, broken routes, overlong handoffs, and
private bytes leaking through an apparently convenient transfer. Its ticket
vocabulary and Europa operating architecture are local choices, not project
defaults.

## Mature source and software projects

**Seatankers** is the clearest source of placement as the dual of search. It
uses Maps for routes, Notes for claims, ADRs for consequential rationale,
source records and tiers for evidence, planning surfaces for durable intent,
and code for repeated exact behavior. Graph checks make objective routing rules
visible. Its full information and ingest architecture would burden a small
project; even here, named owners and checks have sometimes drifted from live
practice.

**Odin** separates a context graph from a research graph and gives research
entities one physical home with several routes. Cases, Notes, Reports, source
manifests, custody rules, accepted-artifact states, ADR tombstones, plans, and a
tracker each answer different questions at its scale. It demonstrates that a
large tracker and instruction set can themselves become stale, and that
exact source revisions and supersession across every route matter.

## Infrastructure and tooling

**dotfiles** separates semantic policy, configuration, procedure, generated
harness projections, and verification. The repository owns a public management
entrypoint while reusable global skills remain replaceable. Upstream provenance
records both the source revision and intentional local divergence. Its
in-progress, active, and archive skill states are a useful maturity boundary.
Unlike the vault projects, dotfiles is CLI-oriented and ordinary Markdown links
fit better than Obsidian wikilinks.

## Deliberately not generalized

Jobs and Server contain useful architectures for their own domains, but the
review did not establish their proposed structures as recurring project
patterns. Keep those decisions in their repositories. Do not use them as
evidence for a universal project scaffold unless future, genuinely different
projects independently reveal the same pressure.

## Evidence basis

This casebook was distilled in August 2026 from read-only reviews of the live
instructions, architectural documents, and representative content in Training,
Cooking, MIT, Hub, Seatankers, Odin, dotfiles, Jobs, and Server. It records why
ideas appeared, not a promise that any checkout remains unchanged.
