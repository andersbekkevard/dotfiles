# Editorial repertoire

Reach for the branch whose visual grammar matches the subject. These are
directions, not templates.

## Comparison and alternatives

Use a shared visual axis for before and after, competing directions, rankings,
or tradeoffs. Side-by-side figures, repeated small multiples, and paired
semantic blocks make differences visible. A borrow/leave or strength/liability
pair works when it records a real editorial judgment.

Inspiration: [code approaches](https://github.com/anthropics/html-effectiveness/blob/main/01-exploration-code-approaches.html) and [visual directions](https://github.com/anthropics/html-effectiveness/blob/main/02-exploration-visual-designs.html).

## Flows and mechanisms

Use inline SVG when exact placement, annotation, or visual weight carries the
meaning. Use Mermaid when the relationship is naturally graph-shaped and its
automatic layout serves the story. A system flow normally starts with files,
packages, modules, or actors; add method detail when sequence is material.

Inspiration: [SVG illustrations](https://github.com/anthropics/html-effectiveness/blob/main/10-svg-illustrations.html) and the [annotated flowchart](https://github.com/anthropics/html-effectiveness/blob/main/13-flowchart-diagram.html).

## Research and explanation

Lead with the mental model, then show the route, mechanism, evidence, and
practical consequence. Use overview first and details on demand when supporting
material would otherwise interrupt the explanation. Keep source locations and
confidence beside the claim they support.

Inspiration: [feature explainer](https://github.com/anthropics/html-effectiveness/blob/main/14-research-feature-explainer.html) and [concept explainer](https://github.com/anthropics/html-effectiveness/blob/main/15-research-concept-explainer.html).

## Communication artifacts

For a status report, incident report, meeting briefing, or recommendation,
shape the sections around the decisions the reader faces. Lead with the answer,
use figures to establish why, and close with the questions or actions that
follow.

Inspiration: [status report](https://github.com/anthropics/html-effectiveness/blob/main/11-status-report.html), [incident report](https://github.com/anthropics/html-effectiveness/blob/main/12-incident-report.html), and [slide deck](https://github.com/anthropics/html-effectiveness/blob/main/09-slide-deck.html).

## Interactive decisions

Use selection, ranking, filtering, triage, or tuning when Anders benefits from
exploring consequences or returning a decision. Keep controls beside their
effect, preserve manual focus, and provide a copy action that exports concise
Markdown, JSON, CSV, TSV, or another faithful format for pasting back to the
agent.

Inspiration: [triage board](https://github.com/anthropics/html-effectiveness/blob/main/18-editor-triage-board.html), [feature flags](https://github.com/anthropics/html-effectiveness/blob/main/19-editor-feature-flags.html), and [prompt tuner](https://github.com/anthropics/html-effectiveness/blob/main/20-editor-prompt-tuner.html).
